## Full description

Bookzenvo keeps service businesses organised wherever the working day takes them.

Use one workspace to view and manage your calendar, bookings, customers, staff, services, stock and reports. Review appointment details, update booking statuses, follow payment progress and keep the booking page and business settings close at hand.

Bookzenvo is designed for salons, studios and other appointment-based businesses that want a clear, polished way to run the day without switching between disconnected tools.

Highlights include:

- A team calendar and complete booking list
- Customer histories and contact details
- Staff, professional and service management
- Stock and business reporting
- Booking payment status and Stripe-powered payment flows
- Notifications, page controls, workspace settings and help

An existing Bookzenvo business account is required. Payment card details are handled securely by Stripe and are not stored in the Bookzenvo app.
