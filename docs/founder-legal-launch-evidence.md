# Founder and adviser launch evidence register

Status date: 27 September 2026. This register lists evidence that cannot be created or proved by application code. It is not legal advice or a declaration that Bookzenvo is ready to launch.

## How to close an item

For every item, keep an access-controlled record containing the evidence file or dashboard link, the date checked, the responsible owner, the independent reviewer where required, the decision, and the next review date. Do not put payment-card data, credentials, recovery codes, database exports, customer health data or provider secrets in this repository.

A screenshot on its own is weak evidence. Keep the underlying agreement, receipt, provider event ID, report or signed decision as well. Any exception needs a named owner, an expiry date and a written launch decision.

## 5. Physical pilot and launch rehearsal

Retain a signed pilot agreement or instruction from the salon, the controller/processor role decision, the salon's privacy information and policies, named operational contacts, the tested release identifier and a dated go/no-go record.

Use fictional or specifically authorised data for rehearsal. Do not copy real customer details, health information or screenshots into this repository. Follow [`pasha-hair-pilot.md`](./pasha-hair-pilot.md), and retain evidence that:

- an actual iPhone and Android phone complete the public booking journey with correct prices, times, policies and contact details;
- desktop owner/staff journeys work for calendar, booking, cancellation, rescheduling, consultation, payment, refund and customer portal features that will be enabled;
- email and SMS arrive once, failures alert the operator, and suppression/opt-out works;
- keyboard-only, 200% and 400% zoom, screen-reader, colour-contrast and mobile orientation checks have been completed;
- support, incident escalation, fallback diary, status communication, deployment rollback and provider recovery contacts work; and
- the salon owner, Bookzenvo launch owner and technical reviewer sign the go/no-go record, including any disabled features and expiry date for temporary exceptions.

Use [`support-on-call-and-pilot-operations.md`](./support-on-call-and-pilot-operations.md) for coverage, severity, conference-day handling and drill evidence. Use [`assisted-salon-migration-runbook.md`](./assisted-salon-migration-runbook.md) before offering a done-for-you import. Reconcile all published and spoken promises with [`launch-scope-and-claims-register.md`](./launch-scope-and-claims-register.md).

## 6. External launch gates

Do not record a gate as complete from source code alone.

- Grant terms and the London conference obligations have been reviewed and calendared.
- Company address, VAT position and website disclosures have been verified from authoritative records.
- The IP ownership chain and every public asset right are evidenced.
- ICO, ROPA, DPIA, lawful-basis, retention, incident and rights-request records are complete.
- The final Terms, Privacy notice, DPA and consumer policies have been reviewed against the live service by qualified advisers.
- Every enabled production provider has passed the activation tests above.
- Backup coverage includes database and Storage, and the restore rehearsal has succeeded.
- The physical salon and mobile pilot has a signed go/no-go outcome.

The final launch record should link each item above, name the person who accepted it and state the exact release and production configuration reviewed. Any later material change to providers, pricing, health/child flows, AI use, grant claims or customer terms reopens the affected gate.
