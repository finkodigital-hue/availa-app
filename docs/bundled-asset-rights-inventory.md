The installed packages report `OFL-1.1` for Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans, Fraunces and Inter, and `ISC` for Lucide React. Retain their licence notices and check any externally loaded fonts separately. Package metadata does not establish rights to the bundled photographs or marketing artwork.

No bundled image remains without a recorded source or a reproducible project source file. Keep this inventory current whenever a public asset is added or replaced. This records provenance; it does not replace contributor assignments or legal advice about ownership and registrable rights.

Ten unreferenced files were removed on 27 September 2026 to reduce the unresolved rights and deployment surface: six unused Pasha demo portraits, three superseded landing screenshots and one unused consultation photograph. Three hard-coded Pasha premises placeholders were also removed because their source rights were unrecorded and presenting illustrative premises on a real salon page could mislead customers. The three unverified Testshop fallback images were removed as well. Every workspace can publish genuine images through the normal gallery after permission is confirmed. The removal does not establish rights in the assets that remain live.
