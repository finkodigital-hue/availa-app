# Salon-owner journey test

Purpose: learn whether Bookzenvo makes switching, booking, checkout and support easier than an owner's current process. Do not describe a feature as validated until real owners have tried it.

## Recruit

Speak to 6–8 UK salon owners: at least two solo, two with multiple stylists, and two who considered switching systems but stayed. Ask for permission to observe their workflow. Use fictional customer details in Bookzenvo and avoid recording client medical information, payment details or private contact details.

## Ask each owner to show and try

1. Show the last awkward booking or diary change in their current system. Then book a simple haircut on a phone in Bookzenvo. Repeat with a colour service that needs a consultation or patch test. If no time appears, try requesting an opening.
2. Show how they currently finish an appointment, take a payment and book the next visit. Repeat in Bookzenvo with a test appointment. Explain that a simulated card reader is not a live payment option.
3. Show the client, service and upcoming-appointment records they would need before switching. Walk through the import review and ask what evidence would make them comfortable changing their booking link. Never import live customer data for this interview without a separate agreement.
4. Find the full monthly cost, payment and messaging charges, then find a human support route for a failed payment or missing booking.

For every task, record elapsed time, taps/clicks, wrong turns, help needed, and the owner's confidence from 1–5. Ask: “Would you trust this with next week's real diary? Why or why not?” Record the exact obstacle, not just a feature request.

## Decision rule

Fix any lost-booking, wrong-payment or consent error before a pilot. Prioritise other issues that recur with at least two owners, especially ones that block a complete journey. Re-test the changed journey with two people who did not see the first version. Keep the results in a separate private research file, not in this repository if they contain personal information.
