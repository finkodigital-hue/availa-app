# Controller, processor and DPA workbook

Status: founder/adviser template. It is not a signed Data Processing Agreement and is not legal advice. A UK data-protection adviser or solicitor must review the completed role analysis and contract before production salon data is accepted.

## Role and responsibility matrix

The role depends on the particular processing purpose; one organisation can have different roles for different activities.

| Activity | Proposed salon role | Proposed BOOKZENVO LTD role | Decision and evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salon client, appointment, staff, service and consultation records | Controller | Processor acting on documented instructions | Salon purpose/lawful basis, categories, retention and authorised users; service instructions and DPA. |
| Booking confirmations, reminders and review requests sent for a salon | Controller | Processor and communications provider coordinator | Approved message purposes, recipients, consent/legitimate-interest analysis, suppression and delivery instructions. |
| Bookzenvo account administration, security, fraud prevention and support | Separate/independent controllers as applicable | Controller for Bookzenvo's own purpose | Privacy-notice entry, lawful basis, access and retention. Adviser confirms any joint purpose. |
| Bookzenvo subscription billing | Customer/business controller for its contact data where applicable | Controller for Bookzenvo billing; Stripe has roles under its terms | Provider terms, tax/invoice retention and privacy notice. |
| Appointment payments facilitated for salons | Salon and Stripe roles need transaction-specific review | Bookzenvo role needs contract/flow review | Stripe Connect agreement, flow of funds, disputes, refunds and privacy disclosures. |
| Optional AI features | Salon controller for workspace/client content | Processor when submitting on salon instruction; potentially controller for limited security/usage records | Feature-specific instruction, minimisation, provider terms, transfers, retention and opt-in/enablement. |
| Product analytics or advertising, if later introduced | To be assessed | Usually Bookzenvo controller | New assessment and notice/consent controls before use. None should be inferred from this template. |
| Legal claims, incident investigation or compliance disclosures | Depends on context | Controller for its legal/security purpose where applicable | Case-specific necessity, lawful basis, access, disclosure and retention decision. |

## Article 28 contract review

Counsel should verify the executed contract covers:

- processing only on documented instructions and notice if an instruction appears unlawful;
- confidentiality obligations for authorised personnel;
- appropriate technical and organisational measures;
- general or specific subprocessor authorisation, advance notice of changes and a workable objection route;
- equivalent subprocessor obligations and continuing processor responsibility;
- reasonable assistance with rights requests, security, breach assessment, DPIAs and prior consultation;
- notification of a personal-data breach without undue delay, with information and updates sufficient for the controller's deadline;
- deletion or return at the controller's choice after service end, copies/backups and lawful retention exceptions;
- information, audit and inspection rights that are practical for a small service and controller;
- international-transfer mechanism and cooperation; and
- priority between the DPA, service terms and any salon instructions.

## Operational split

| Task | Salon | Bookzenvo | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tell clients about salon processing | Own and publish salon notice | Provide configurable page fields and platform notice | URL/version/screenshot without client data |
| Choose lawful basis and Article 9 condition | Decide and document | Do not choose on salon's behalf; provide product controls | Salon decision/DPIA |
| User access and leavers | Approve, review and remove users | Enforce configured roles; support recovery | Quarterly access review |
| Rights request | Receive, verify, decide exemptions and communicate | Supply processor assistance/export/deletion tooling | Request log and completion evidence |
| Incident | Escalate suspected salon misuse promptly | Contain, investigate, notify controller and preserve evidence | Incident record |
| Retention | Set defensible salon schedule | Apply available controls and provider deletion processes | Schedule and test |
| Import | Confirm authority, source and accuracy | Process only approved file; secure transfer and cleanup | Signed migration instruction |
| Service termination | Choose return/export and deletion route | Export/return and delete under contract, subject to recorded exceptions | Closure certificate/reference |

## Signature gate

Record the executed agreement version, effective date, authorised signatories, salon privacy-contact details, security schedule, subprocessor-register version and any negotiated exceptions. Product acceptance should record the version and timestamp; an unsigned repository template is not evidence of agreement.
